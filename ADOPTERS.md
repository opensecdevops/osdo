# OSDO Adopters

This file records organizations and teams that publicly confirm they use OSDO in development, security, CI/CD, platform engineering, training, evaluation, or production environments.

The purpose of this registry is to provide verifiable evidence of adoption without implying endorsement, certification, sponsorship, or formal affiliation.

## Confirmed adopters

_No external adopters have been publicly confirmed in this registry yet._

## How to be listed

An organization or team may request inclusion through a public issue or pull/merge request containing:

- organization or team name;
- public website or repository when available;
- OSDO components being used;
- usage context, such as evaluation, development, training, internal platform, CI/CD or production;
- a contact or public reference capable of confirming the adoption statement;
- whether the organization authorizes use of its name in OSDO project materials.

Sensitive implementation details are not required.

## Adoption status

Where useful, entries may use one of the following non-certification labels:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Evaluation | OSDO is being technically evaluated or piloted |
| Development | OSDO is used in development or non-production workflows |
| Training | OSDO is used for education, workshops or internal enablement |
| Internal | OSDO is used in internal engineering/security workflows |
| Production | OSDO participates in a production software-delivery process |

These labels describe self-reported use only. They are not OSDO certifications.

| Organization / Team | Status | Components | Public reference | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Verification and removal

Maintainers may request evidence before accepting an entry. Entries may be removed or marked historical if they can no longer be verified.

OSDO maintainers must not add an organization solely because it sponsored, collaborated with, or attended an OSDO/Hacker Dreams activity. Adoption and sponsorship are separate concepts.
